Privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

Kafei (كفي) is an online shop in Kuwait selling coffee machines, grinders, beans and brewing gear at kafeikw.com. This page explains what information we keep about you, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

What we collect

  • When you order: your name, phone number, delivery address and the items you bought.
  • When you create an account: your email address, the username you choose and your password. Your password is stored in scrambled form by our sign-in provider; we can't read it.
  • When you sign in with Google: the name and email address on your Google account. We don't receive your Google password or anything else from your Google account.
  • When you pay online: KNET, card and Apple Pay payments are handled by MyFatoorah on their secure page. We never see or store your card number. We only receive whether the payment went through.

How we use it

  • To deliver your order and contact you about it, by phone or WhatsApp.
  • To show your past orders and saved address in your account, so checkout is faster next time.
  • To send the codes that confirm your email and reset your password.

We don't sell your information, and we don't send marketing emails unless you ask us to.

Who else handles it

  • Supabase stores our database and customer accounts.
  • MyFatoorah processes online payments.
  • Google, only if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Hostinger hosts the website.

Cookies and your browser

We save your cart, your language choice and your sign-in in your own browser so they're still there when you come back. We don't use advertising trackers.

Your choices

You can change your saved details in your account at any time. To have your account and order history deleted, email info@kafeikw.com or message us on WhatsApp at +965 6690 8822. We keep records of paid orders only as long as Kuwaiti law requires.

Contact

Kafei, Kuwait. info@kafeikw.com
