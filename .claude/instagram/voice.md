## The name and the story (use it, it's the brand)

In the Gulf, when you've had enough Arabic coffee, you shake your finjan (the small cup) and the host stops pouring. Kafei (كفي) means "enough". The finjan with a little shake is the logo. Lines that play on "enough", the finjan, the host who keeps pouring, and the majlis feel like us.

## My positions

Things Kafei believes that some of the audience does not. (confirm all)

1. Fresh beans matter more than an expensive machine. A good grinder and beans with a roast date beat a fancy machine with old supermarket coffee.
2. You don't need a café to drink café-level coffee. A V60, a kettle and a grinder is enough to start.
3. A short list is better than a huge catalogue. We sell fewer things that we'd actually use.
4. Specialty coffee can taste like fruit and candy (grape, peach, bubble gum, blueberry) with nothing added. That's the bean, not a syrup.
5. Ask before you buy. The right machine depends on your counter, your drinks and your budget, and we'll tell you honestly, even if it's the cheaper one.
