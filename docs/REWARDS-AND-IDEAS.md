# Kafei: reward programme (agreed) and improvement ideas
Saved 17 November 2026. Not built yet.

## Reward programme: "Another cup" (فنجان ثاني)
Name from the shop's story: in the Gulf you shake your finjan when you've had enough; until then the host keeps pouring.
Why it works: gear is bought once, beans every few weeks. The programme turns machine buyers into bean buyers, then rewards the habit.

1. **Points on everything:** 1 point per KWD 1. 100 points = KWD 3 off (3% back). Points expire after 6 months without an order.
2. **Bean card:** buy 5 bags, the 6th is free. Shown as 6 finjan icons in the account, filling up.
3. **Machine buyers start with beans credit:** every machine or grinder includes a KWD 5 beans voucher, valid 30 days.
4. **Levels (spend in the last 12 months):**
   | Level | Threshold | Perks |
   |---|---|---|
   | Guest (ضيف) | sign-up | 1 pt per KWD, bean card |
   | Regular (معزّب) | KWD 100 | 1.5 pts per KWD, free delivery on every order |
   | Family (أهل البيت) | KWD 300 | 2 pts per KWD, early access to new arrivals and limited beans, birthday gift |
5. **Bonuses:** sign-up 50 pts; refer a friend: friend gets KWD 3 off, referrer gets 100 pts after the friend's first order; product review 20 pts; birthday month double points.
6. **Reminders:** restock nudge based on each customer's buying rhythm ("1 bag from a free one"); points expiry warning 2 weeks ahead. Sent by WhatsApp or email.

Cost estimate: about 3–5% of sales; the free 6th bag is about 16% off beans for repeat buyers only; KWD 5 per machine sale.
To build: points and bean card in the customer account, "use my points" at checkout, rewards view in the dashboard, reminder messages. Every number editable from the dashboard.

## Improvement ideas (most sales first)
**Sell more**
1. Reward programme (above).
2. "Notify me when it's back" on sold-out colours and coming-soon categories.
3. Bundles (e.g. xBloom + grinder + beans at a small discount).
4. Free-delivery progress bar in the cart ("Add KWD 3.000 more for free delivery").
5. Deema "pay in 4" line under prices, once Deema approves.

**Trust**
6. Reviews after delivery, shown on product pages.
7. Warranty and returns page.
8. "Genuine from authorised supplier" badge, once supplier letters are in hand.

**Content for Google**
9. Brew guides in English and Arabic, linking to the gear.
10. Side-by-side product comparison (grinders, machines).

**Shopping**
11. Search in the header (English and Arabic).
12. Filters on category pages (price, brand, manual/electric).
13. Wishlist in the customer account.
14. WhatsApp order updates (received, out for delivery, delivered).

**Back office**
15. Low-stock alert to the owner.
16. Sales report in the dashboard (best sellers, weekly revenue, repeat customers).
17. Discount codes with date ranges.

**Messaging**
18. AI replies on WhatsApp and Instagram: Arabic or English, live prices, stock and delivery from the dashboard, order status, checkout links; hands complaints, refunds, wholesale and anything uncertain to the owner; the owner can take over any chat. Needs: verified Meta Business account, WhatsApp Business Platform, Instagram Business account linked to the Facebook Page, an AI API key. Start with Instagram (quicker, free), then WhatsApp once Meta verifies the business.
