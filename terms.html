Last updated 22 November 2026

Prices

Prices are in Kuwaiti dinars (KWD). The price you pay is the one shown at checkout when you pay. For items marked "price on request", we confirm the price on WhatsApp before you pay.

Orders and payment

  • You can pay by KNET, Visa, Mastercard or Apple Pay. Payments go through MyFatoorah; we never see or keep your card details.
  • Your order is confirmed once the payment succeeds, and you see its order number on screen.
  • If an item turns out to be unavailable after you've paid, we contact you and refund it in full, or offer a replacement if you prefer.

Delivery

  • We deliver across Kuwait. Orders placed before 2 pm reach the Capital and Hawalli the same day; for other areas we confirm the delivery day with you.
  • Delivery costs KWD 1.500, and is free on orders over KWD 15.
  • Please check your order when it arrives. If anything is damaged, missing or wrong, tell us within 48 hours with a photo, and we'll put it right.

Returns and refunds

You can return unused items in their original packaging within 14 days. The details, including what can't be returned, are on our returns and exchanges page.

Warranty

Machines and grinders come with the manufacturer's warranty where one applies. If something stops working, message us and we'll help with the claim.

Your account

Keep your password private. You're responsible for orders placed from your account, so tell us straight away if you think someone else has used it. How we handle your information is explained in our privacy policy.

The law

These terms follow the laws of the State of Kuwait.
